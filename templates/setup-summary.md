# Setup messages (what the owner sees)

These are the only things the owner sees during setup. Plain words, no file names, folder names, IDs or settings. Bold titles, short bullets, a divider between sections, no emoji.
Fill the `{braces}`. Leave out a line you have nothing real for rather than inventing it.

---

## 1. Ask for the website

```
**Connected to {workspace name}.** Is that the right workspace?

**What's your website?**
I'll read it to learn what you do and who you should be emailing. That's all I need to get started.
```

If they have no website: ask four short questions instead, one message: what you sell, who it's for, what you'd like people to do after reading your email, and your best result so far.

---

## 2. Show what you understood

```
**Here's what I understood about {company}**

---

**What you do**
{one or two plain sentences}

**Who you help**
{one sentence}

**Who I'd email first**
- **Roles:** {three job titles}
- **Companies:** {type and size}
- **Where:** {places, or "anywhere"}
- **I'd leave out:** {who not to contact}

**What you offer, and what I'd ask for**
- **Offer:** {what you sell, in a line}
- **The ask:** {a short call | a yes to send case studies | a reply with a time}

**Results I may mention**
- {exact result} (from your {page})
- {exact result} (from your {page})
{or: I didn't find specific results on your site, so I won't mention any until you give me some.}

**How you sound**
{three words}. For example: "{a short line from the site}"

**Your Instantly**
{N} campaigns and {M} sending accounts. I'll look after: {campaign names}.

---

**Want to change anything?** Say "looks good", or tell me what to change. For example: "target agencies with 10 or more clients".
```

Mark anything you guessed as "(my guess)". Quote results exactly as the site words them.

---

## 3. Fill gaps (only what is missing, three questions at most, each with a suggestion)

```
**Three quick things:**
1. **How do people book a call with you?** {a link I found, or "I couldn't find one"}
2. **Which city are you in?** (so I get times right) {my guess}
3. **Which campaigns should I look after?** {names}. I'll take all of them unless you say otherwise.
```

---

## 4. Ready to save

```
**Ready to save?**
I'll keep this on my computer. Nothing will be sent: in practice mode I only draft, and I ask you first.

Say "save", or tell me what to change.
```

---

## 5. All set

```
**You're all set, {first name}.**

**How I'll work**
- I draft, you decide. I never send anything on my own.
- I ask before anything that costs credits or changes something in Instantly.
- Say "stop" any time and I stop.

{the menu from templates/menu.md}
```

If some sending accounts have no name in Instantly, add before the menu:

```
**One thing to fix:** {n} of your sending accounts have no name set in Instantly ({addresses}). I sign replies with the sending account's name, so add one there, or tell me what to sign those with.
```
